# Absorbed into Unit 4

These were the old **Unit 4: Building and Trusting a Model** and old **Unit 5: Prediction and
Its Uncertainty**. They are now one unit, `Slides/Unit_04_Prediction_and_Choosing_Predictors.tex`,
and everything after them shifted down by one, so the course is 11 units.

Nothing here is taught as it stands. It is kept because the merged deck took the core and left
some material behind, and this is where to look for it.

## What carried over into the new Unit 4

- predicting a new case, and the two kinds of interval around a prediction
- extrapolation
- error on your own rows versus error on held-out rows
- train/test splits and K-fold cross-validation
- underfitting and overfitting, and the complexity curve
- comparing candidate sets of predictors by AIC, BIC, and cross-validated error
- searching through variables as a form of overfitting, and penalized regression

## What did not carry over

From the old Unit 4:

- interactions, transformations, and multicollinearity (interactions are covered in Unit 3)
- trees, random forests, and boosting in any detail, and feature importance
- the one-standard-error rule as a named rule

From the old Unit 5:

- the four-layer breakdown of prediction error (noise, estimation, model error, shift)
- calibration
- distribution shift
- regression to the mean as its own treatment (it is in Unit 3)

The code companions, homework, and practice questions for both old units. The new Unit 4 needs
